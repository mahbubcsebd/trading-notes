# Risk Management: The Rules for Staying Alive

A car has brakes not only to stop, but to give you the confidence to drive fast. With good brakes you can drive on a mountain road. In trading, risk management is that brake.

## What is risk management

**Risk management means deciding in advance how much you are willing to lose on each trade, each day and across the whole account, and then sticking to that limit.**

This chapter answers one question: **"If I am wrong, how much will I lose, and how do I stay in the game after that loss?"**

## Why learn this now

- **Learn the exit before the entry.** You already know [Market Structure](/en/learn/03-market-structure/00-overview), [Support and Resistance](/en/learn/05-support-resistance/00-overview) and [Indicators](/en/learn/07-technical-indicators/00-overview). But even a good entry is sometimes wrong.
- **Everyone loses.** The difference is that some people stop at a small loss and others wipe out the account with a big one.
- **All later chapters stand on this one.** [Trade Management](/en/learn/10-trade-management-execution/01-stop-loss-placement), [Psychology](/en/learn/12-trading-psychology/01-discipline) and the [Trading Plan](/en/learn/14-trading-plan-system/01-trading-plan-template) assume the rules from here.
- **A wrong calculation is the most expensive mistake.** Even a good strategy can wipe out an account at too large a size.

## What you will be able to do after this chapter

- **Set the risk per trade in money.**
- **Calculate the lot size** from your stop.
- **Check the Risk:Reward** before you take a trade.
- **Write your drawdown rules** in advance.
- **Set daily and weekly loss limits.**
- **Multiply correlated positions together** to measure total risk.
- **Use expectancy and risk of ruin** to tell whether a system will survive.

## How these lessons work together

Before a trade, the questions come in this order:

1. **Can I trade today?** The daily and weekly limits (lesson 5) and the drawdown rules (lesson 4) tell you.
2. **How much risk?** Risk per trade (lesson 1) sets it in money.
3. **What other positions are open?** Correlation (lesson 6) shows the total risk.
4. **What is the ratio?** Risk:Reward (lesson 3) tells you if the trade is worth taking.
5. **How many lots?** Position sizing (lesson 2) works out the lots from the stop.
6. **Will the whole system survive?** Expectancy and risk of ruin (lessons 7-8) check this over the long run.

## How to read this chapter

- **Read in order.** Each lesson builds on the one before.
- **Do the math yourself.** Take a calculator and rework every example.
- **Practice on a demo account.** Not with real money.
- **Change the numbers to fit your account.** The 10,000 in the examples may not be your account.
