# Trading Psychology: A Roadmap to Winning the Fight With Yourself

The car is good and the road is familiar, but if the driver falls asleep there is an accident. In trading, strategy and risk math are the car and the road. You are the driver. This chapter is about the driver.

## What is trading psychology

**Trading psychology is the habit of making decisions by rules written in advance, even in the middle of profit, loss, fear and greed.**

This chapter answers one question: **"Even when I know the rules, why do I break them, and how do I stop?"**

## Why learn this now

- **The rules of the earlier chapters get broken by emotion.** Moving the [stop](/en/learn/10-trade-management-execution/01-stop-loss-placement), raising the size, entering outside the plan: all of these are work of the mind.
- **The same mistake happens again and again.** If you do not know its name, you cannot catch it.
- **Even a good strategy loses if it is run badly.** [Expectancy](/en/learn/08-risk-management/07-expectancy) works only when the rules are followed.
- **The next chapter stands on this one.** The [Trading Plan](/en/learn/14-trading-plan-system/01-trading-plan-template) is a document you will follow in a moment of emotion.

## What you will be able to do after this chapter

- **Define discipline** through specific behaviors.
- **Catch and stop overtrading.**
- **Accept a loss** as a normal cost.
- **Recognize the signals of FOMO and revenge trading** and stop.
- **Keep a journal** and find the pattern of your mistakes.
- **Arrange a daily routine.**

## How these lessons work together

In a day, the questions come in this order:

1. **When do I sit down, and when do I get up?** Set the routine (lesson 6).
2. **Do I take the trade or not?** Check the signals of overtrading and FOMO (lessons 2, 4).
3. **Am I following the plan?** Entry, stop and target written in advance, unchanged while the trade runs (lesson 1).
4. **If I lose?** Treat it as a normal cost and wait for the next setup (lessons 3, 4).
5. **At the end of the day?** Write it in the journal with reasons (lesson 5).

- Practice following the rules on a demo account first.
