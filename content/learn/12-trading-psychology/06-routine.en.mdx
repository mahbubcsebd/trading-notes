# Trading Routine: Arrange the Day and Decisions Get Better

Some days you trade as soon as you wake up, some days at three at night, some days you sit at the chart all day. Then you wonder why your decisions are so scattered. This lesson is about arranging the day.

## What you will be able to do

- **Divide the day** into three parts: **preparation, trade window and review**.
- **Set a trade window.**
- **Keep a rule of not trading outside the window.**

## Why it matters

- **A tired mind makes mistakes.** Sleep, food and breaks keep the quality of decisions.
- **More attention in less time.** [Overtrading](/en/learn/12-trading-psychology/02-overtrading) falls.
- **Habit makes rules easier.** The same time, the same work, less decision fatigue.

Prerequisites: [Journaling Habit](/en/learn/12-trading-psychology/05-journaling-habit) and [Trading Sessions](/en/learn/01-foundations/05-trading-sessions).

## What is a routine

**A trading routine is a list of tasks done in the same order every day: preparation, a trade window at a set time, and a review at the end.**

Think of the school bell. Study time, play time, break. When the bell rings, everyone knows what to do now without discussion. Your routine is your bell.

| Step         | What to do                                                                                                               | Time (example)               |
| :----------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------- |
| Preparation  | Look at the calendar, check [news](/en/learn/11-fundamental-intermarket-analysis/06-news-trade-avoid-korbo), mark levels | 30 minutes before the window |
| Trade window | Only the plan's setups                                                                                                   | For example 2 hours          |
| Review       | Write the journal, screenshots                                                                                           | 15 minutes after the window  |
| Break        | Chart closed                                                                                                             | The rest of the day          |

The times are examples. Set them to match your time zone, work and strategy.

## Step by step: building a routine

<Steps>

### Step 1: Pick your window

A time when you are alert and the market has movement. For example, the two hours after London opens.

### Step 2: Write the preparation list

News, levels, the day's trade limit, today's risk. On one page.

### Step 3: The rule for the window

Only the plan's setups inside the window. When it ends, close the platform.

### Step 4: Review

Fill the journal, tag the mistakes, and one goal for tomorrow.

### Step 5: Life outside trading

Sleep, food, exercise. These are part of trading, not luxury.

</Steps>

## Example 1: when it works (synthetic)

- **Set in advance:** a two-hour window, at most two trades, risk 1%.
- **While running:** one A+ setup inside the window. Buy 1.0935, stop 1.0905, target 1.1000.
- **Result:** the target was hit. After the window the trader wrote the journal and got up.
- **Lesson:** a fixed time keeps attention sharp, and the mind rests the rest of the time.

(Synthetic educational example. This is not a real trade.)

- **Why it failed:** the window was closed. The trade was outside the plan, and the reason was boredom.
- **Lesson:** a closed window means the work is done. A trade outside it is usually emotional.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake                       | Why it happens            | What to do                              |
| :---------------------------- | :------------------------ | :-------------------------------------- |
| All day in front of the chart | Fear of missing something | Use alerts, limit the window            |
| Trading without preparation   | Hurry                     | A 30-minute preparation list            |
| Trading on little sleep       | Time pressure             | If tired, skip that day                 |
| Skipping the review           | Laziness                  | Fix 15 minutes at the end of the window |

## Pro tips

<Callout type="tip">
  Close the platform at the end of the window, or log out of the mobile app.
  When removing it is easy, "let me just look once" happens less.
</Callout>

<Callout type="note">
  Test your routine: run it for two weeks and see at which time your decisions
  are good. Then change the window.
</Callout>

## When this does not work

- **If the window does not fit your work hours:** the rule will not last. Take a realistic time.
- **For swing or position trading:** the window is short, but preparation and review matter more.
- **At times of news risk:** even if there is a window, the news rules come first.

## Summary

<KeyTakeaways>

- A routine has three parts: preparation, window, review.
- A trade outside the window is usually emotional.
- Sleep and breaks are part of trading.
- You can test and change the routine, but not while a trade is running.

</KeyTakeaways>

## Practice

Write a daily routine for next week: preparation time, window, review time, break. Each day, tick in the journal how much of it you followed.
