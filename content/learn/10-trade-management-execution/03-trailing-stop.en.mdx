# Trailing Stop: A Rule for Protecting Running Profit

The trade is going well and profit is growing. Now there are two fears: leaving early and missing the big move, or waiting too long and giving the profit back. A trailing stop is the road between the two.

## What you will be able to do

- **Explain how** a trailing stop works.
- **Move the stop** using structure.
- **Understand the harm** of a trail that is too close, and set your own distance.

## Why it matters

- **Protect profit and let the move run, both at once.** The stop goes up and the trade keeps running.
- **A rule written earlier reduces emotion.** The thought "let me get out now" turns into a rule.
- **A wrong trail costs money.** If it is too close you leave the move. If it is too far you give the profit back.

Prerequisites: [Take Profit and Partial Close](/en/learn/10-trade-management-execution/02-take-profit-partial-close) and [ATR](/en/learn/07-technical-indicators/04-atr-stop-size).

## What is a trailing stop

**A trailing stop is a stop that moves along as soon as price goes in the direction of profit, but never moves the other way.**

Think of climbing stairs. The step before the one you are stepping on is your safe place. If you slip, you stop at the earlier step, not all the way down.

| Method          | How it moves                              | Benefit                         |
| :-------------- | :---------------------------------------- | :------------------------------ |
| Structure trail | Below each new higher low                 | Follows the shape of the move   |
| ATR trail       | A multiple of ATR below the highest price | Matches the speed of the market |
| Moving average  | When price closes below the MA            | Simple, trend-based             |

## Step by step: the structure trail

<Steps>

### Step 1: Place the starting stop

[Beyond the structure](/en/learn/10-trade-management-execution/01-stop-loss-placement). Trailing starts once the trade is in profit.

### Step 2: Wait for a new higher low

Let price go up, pull back and form a new [higher low](/en/learn/03-market-structure/02-trend-range-hhhl).

### Step 3: Move the stop

A little below the new higher low. Leave a buffer for noise and spread.

### Step 4: Never backward

The stop moves only toward profit. Even if price falls, the stop does not fall.

### Step 5: Exit when the stop is hit

Leaving in profit is the rule. Not the thought "a little more".

</Steps>

## Example 1: when it works (synthetic)

- **Set in advance:** risk 1%, starting stop 30 pips.
- **Result:** the stop rose below two higher lows. Price went high, fell and touched the stop, for a profit of about 64 pips (more than 2R).
- **Lesson:** the structure trail put up with the normal pullbacks and took you out on the real break.

(Synthetic educational example. This is not a real trade.)

## Common mistakes

| Mistake                                  | Why it happens                | What to do                                  |
| :--------------------------------------- | :---------------------------- | :------------------------------------------ |
| Trailing below every candle              | A hurry to save profit        | Use structure or a multiple of ATR          |
| Starting to trail before profit          | Fear                          | First follow the rule for the starting stop |
| Moving the trail backward                | Not wanting to give up profit | The stop moves only one way                 |
| Changing method in the middle of a trade | Uncertainty                   | Pick it first and stick to it to the end    |

## Pro tips

<Callout type="tip">
  Set the trail distance by looking at the size of recent pullbacks. The trail
  should be bigger than the usual pullback in pips.
</Callout>

<Callout type="note">
  A trailing stop and a partial close can be used together: half closed early,
  the rest trailed.
</Callout>

## When this does not work

- **In a range market:** there is no trend, and the trail gets cut again and again.
- **With a gap in a fast move:** the fill may not be at the stop price ([Slippage](/en/learn/10-trade-management-execution/04-slippage-requote-spread-widening)).
- **If the trail is very short:** you exit only on noise.
- **When the trend ends:** profit goes back, so keep a target too.

## Summary

<KeyTakeaways>

- A trailing stop moves toward profit, never backward.
- Trail with structure or ATR, not below every candle.
- A trail that is too close leaves the move, and one that is too far gives profit back.
- Write the trail rule before the trade.

</KeyTakeaways>
