# Retail Forex in Bangladesh: A Framework for Checking Rules and Tax

**This is not legal or tax advice.** Laws and guidance change, and this lesson does not know your particular situation. What it gives you is a list of questions and a way to check them, so that before you decide anything you can confirm the answers yourself **with the right authorities and qualified professionals**.

## What you will be able to do

- **Build** a list of questions you can check.
- **Know** where to check them.
- **Recognise** the risks of informal channels.
- **Understand** the safe way to learn (demo).

## Why it matters

- **Not knowing does not reduce legal risk.** There are rules on sending money to foreign platforms and bringing earnings back.
- **Fraudsters exploit exactly this gap.** People offering an "easy way" are often the danger.
- **There may be tax obligations.** Whether or not you earn anything, keeping records is wise.

Prerequisites: [How to Choose a Broker](/en/learn/02-broker-platform-setup/01-broker-kivabe-choose-korbo) and [Demo vs Live](/en/learn/02-broker-platform-setup/02-demo-vs-live-account).

## What we know, and what we don't

**What we know (in general terms; check it yourself):**

- In Bangladesh, foreign exchange transactions generally fall under the **Foreign Exchange Regulation Act, 1947** and Bangladesh Bank's guidance.
- Bangladesh Bank has at various times **warned the public about trading on foreign online Forex platforms**. See the latest position and the specific circulars on its **official website**.
- Sending or receiving money abroad outside legal banking channels (for example, through hundi) is a serious legal matter.

**What we don't know / what this lesson cannot tell you:**

- Exactly what is allowed and what is not at the time you are reading.
- How Forex earnings are classified under tax law.
- What applies in your personal situation (expatriate, business owner, government employee and so on).

Only **the authorities and professionals** can answer these questions.

## The checking framework

| Question                                                                 | Whom to ask                                                                 |
| :----------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Is it legal to send money to trade on a foreign online Forex platform?   | Bangladesh Bank (official circulars/helpline), or an authorised dealer bank |
| What is the legal way to bring foreign earnings into the country?        | Bangladesh Bank, your bank                                                  |
| How, and under which category, is the income taxed?                      | National Board of Revenue (NBR), a tax adviser                              |
| Are there extra limits in my personal situation (profession, residence)? | A lawyer                                                                    |
| In which country is the broker regulated, and how am I protected there?  | That country's regulator's official site                                    |

Keep every answer **in writing** and **with the date**.

## The risks of informal channels

Offers often come through social media or acquaintances: "Give me the money and I'll deposit it for you" or "Withdraw your money through this person". This brings:

- **Legal risk:** The liability for using an unauthorised channel can fall on you.
- **Fraud risk:** If the money is gone, your legal basis for getting it back is weak.
- **Security:** Your personal details and logins are at risk.

## Step by step: the safe path

<Steps>

### Step 1: Just learn; don't send money

Practising on a demo account costs nothing.

### Step 2: Make your list of questions

Your own questions, from the framework above.

### Step 3: Go to the authorities

Directly to the official website, circulars or office. A social media post is not proof.

### Step 4: Get professional advice

On law and tax, in writing.

### Step 5: Write down your decision

What you learned, from whom, and when. Check again if your situation changes.

</Steps>

## Example 1: when it works (synthetic)

- **Situation:** A student is interested in learning Forex.
- **Action:** They practised only on demo, read the latest guidance on Bangladesh Bank's website, spoke to a lawyer, and wrote down their decision.
- **Result:** Learning continued without putting money at risk; the next step came only once the legal position was clear.
- **Lesson:** Learning and sending money are separate decisions; the first carries no risk.

(Synthetic educational example.)

## Example 2: when it fails (synthetic)

- **Situation:** A social media ad for an "easy Forex deposit/withdrawal" service.
- **Action:** Someone sent money to an unknown agent through personal mobile banking.
- **Result:** No "account" was opened, and the agent stopped responding. The legal options for getting the money back are narrow.
- **Why it failed:** Money sent to a stranger through an informal channel, with no checks.
- **Lesson:** An offer of an "easy way" is the biggest warning sign.

(Synthetic educational example.)

## Common mistakes

| Mistake                                     | Why it happens     | What to do                                |
| :------------------------------------------ | :----------------- | :---------------------------------------- |
| Taking Facebook/YouTube posts as the law    | Easy answers       | Official sources                          |
| Feeling safe because "everyone is doing it" | Social proof       | Your liability is your own                |
| Sending money to an unknown agent           | Convenience        | Never                                     |
| Not keeping tax records                     | Assuming no income | Keep your journal and transaction records |
| Deciding on outdated information            | Rules change       | Check the latest guidance                 |

## When this does not work

- **If you don't live in Bangladesh:** Your own country's rules apply; ask these questions of your country's authorities.
- **If the rules change:** The information in this lesson may be out of date.
- **If your personal situation is complex:** Only a lawyer or tax adviser can help.

## Summary

<KeyTakeaways>

- This is not legal/tax advice; check with official sources and professionals.
- Moving money outside legal channels is risky and possibly illegal.
- Learning on demo does not require sending any money.
- Keep your decisions and answers in writing.

</KeyTakeaways>
