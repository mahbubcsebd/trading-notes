# Broker and Platform Setup: Getting Ready to Start Safely

Before you drive a car, you check your licence, seatbelt and brakes. Trading is similar: before your first trade, a few things need to be in place — a trustworthy broker, a safe setup, the right orders, and a clear picture of the rules in your own country.

## What Broker and Platform Setup covers

**This is the practical preparation before you start trading: where to open an account, how to practise, which tools to work with, and how to keep your account from going past its limits.**

The chapter answers one question: **"How do I start in a safe place, with the right tools, while keeping the risk of mistakes low?"**

## Why learn this now

- **With the wrong broker you can lose everything.** A good strategy is worthless if you cannot withdraw your money.
- **One wrong button can cause a big loss.** The lot size, the order direction, forgetting the stop.
- **Without legal clarity, the risk doubles.** Especially through informal channels.
- **The later chapters need this setup for practice.** (From [Market Structure](/en/learn/03-market-structure/00-overview) onwards, you will use charts on demo.)

## By the end of this chapter you will be able to

- **Check** a broker against seven criteria.
- **Plan** a safe path from demo to live.
- **Set up** MT5 and TradingView and place alerts.
- **Choose** the right order type.
- **Calculate** margin level and stop-out.
- **Build** a framework for checking legal and tax questions.

## How the lessons fit together

The decision flow at the start:

1. **Is the legal position clear?** The questions in Lesson 6 come first.
2. **Check the broker.** Seven criteria (Lesson 1).
3. **Practise on demo.** A realistic balance, conditions written down (Lesson 2).
4. **Set up the platform.** A simple chart, alerts, a second check (Lesson 3).
5. **Choose the order.** Limit or stop, together with a stop loss (Lesson 4).
6. **Know your margin limits.** Start with small risk (Lesson 5).

## How to use this chapter

- Read in order, though it is fine to read Lesson 6 first.
- From Lesson 2 on, open a demo account and do every step hands-on.
- Finish each lesson's practice before you put real money at risk.
- Every chart in this chapter is synthetic, made to explain an idea. They are not real results.
