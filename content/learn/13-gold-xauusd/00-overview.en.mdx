# Gold (XAUUSD): Meeting the Most Restless Thing in the Market

A stop that is enough on EURUSD can be gone in one candle on gold. The same lot, but the amount of money is much bigger. This chapter teaches the rules that belong to gold.

## What is gold (XAUUSD)

**XAUUSD is the price of one troy ounce of gold in US dollars.** "XAU" is the code for gold, and "USD" is the dollar.

This chapter answers one question: **"How do the risk rules that work in forex have to change for gold?"**

## Why learn this now

- **Gold moves a lot.** With the same lot, profit and loss can be much bigger than in forex.
- **Gold is tied to many things.** The dollar, yields, fear and news all push at once.
- **Speed changes with session and news.** If you do not know the timing, your stop gets hit again and again.
- **What you learned in the earlier chapter is useful here.** The [relationship of gold and DXY](/en/learn/11-fundamental-intermarket-analysis/05-correlation-gold-vs-dxy) started there.

## What you will be able to do after this chapter

- **Say what makes the price of gold move.**
- **Work out the value of each $1 move** from the **contract spec**.
- **Read DXY, yields and fear events.**
- **Recognize the risk of sessions and news.**
- **Count spread and slippage** in advance.
- **Set the stop with ATR and work out the lot size.**
- **Check the London breakout setup** by the rules.

## How these lessons work together

Before trading gold, these questions come in order:

1. **What is happening behind gold today?** The dollar, yields, fear (lessons 1, 3, 4).
2. **Which session and which news is it now?** (lessons 5, 6).
3. **What is the cost?** Spread and slippage (lesson 7).
4. **How far is the stop, how many lots?** The stop from ATR, the lot from the stop (lessons 9, 8).
5. **Which setup is it?** Check it by session-based rules (lesson 10).

- Contract spec, spread and session times differ by broker. Check your own broker's information.
