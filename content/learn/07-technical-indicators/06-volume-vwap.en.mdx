# Volume and VWAP: Check Whether a Breakout Has Participation

Price broke a level. Everyone says "breakout!". But how many people actually joined in? A goal in an empty stadium is not like a goal in a full one. Volume measures that crowd.

## What you will be able to do

- Explain what volume tells you and **why tick volume has limits in forex**.
- Look for **volume confirmation** on a breakout.
- Read **VWAP** to see where the average buyer and seller stand.
- **Reduce risk** on a weak-volume breakout.

## Why it matters

- **The force behind price.** Price tells you what happened; volume tells you how many were part of it.
- **A breakout filter.** It works well combined with the third condition of Range Breakout.
- **It avoids wrong expectations.** Forex has no central volume; if you do not know this, you will misread it.

Prerequisites: [Range Breakout](/en/learn/06-trend-chart-patterns/03-range-breakout) and [Moving Averages](/en/learn/07-technical-indicators/01-moving-averages-crossover).

## What are volume and VWAP

**Volume measures how much was traded in a period. VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price) is the average price of that trading, where prices with more volume carry more weight.**

Think of mangoes sold at a market. The price at which the most mangoes sold is the "real" price. VWAP is that average.

**Caution for forex:** spot forex does not trade on a central exchange, so there is no single real volume. What you see on the chart is usually **tick volume**, that is, how many times price changed during that candle. It is an estimate of actual trading, but not a reliable measure. Volume in futures or stocks is more direct.

VWAP is usually calculated from the start of a day. On this lesson's charts we use an "anchored VWAP", which starts from a chosen candle.

## Step by step: checking a breakout with volume

<Steps>

### Step 1: Set the range and level

In the example, the range top is 1.0894.

### Step 2: Compare the breakout candle's volume with the average

Check whether it is clearly higher than the average of the last 20 candles. "Clearly higher" means a big gap by your own judgement, not a small one.

### Step 3: Check the position relative to VWAP

Price above VWAP means recent buyers are in profit.

### Step 4: Place entry and stop

Entry 1.0911. Stop inside the range at 1.0882, a risk of 29 pips.

### Step 5: Set the target

At least twice the risk. In the example, 1.0969.

</Steps>

## Example 1: when it works (synthetic)

The chart above:

- **What you could see:** a high-volume candle breaking the range top, price above VWAP.
- **Entry:** buy 1.0911.
- **Stop:** 1.0882. Risk 29 pips.
- **Target:** 1.0969, about 1:2.
- **Result:** price hit the target.
- **Lesson:** volume raised confidence in the breakout, but the stop was still placed.

(Synthetic educational example. This is not a real trade, and no result is guaranteed.)

The same range, but weak volume on the breakout:

- **Lesson:** weak volume does not guarantee failure, but there is no extra confirmation. Cut the size or wait.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake                                 | Why it happens                 | What to do                           |
| :-------------------------------------- | :----------------------------- | :----------------------------------- |
| Treating tick volume as real volume     | It looks the same on the chart | Remember its limits                  |
| Picking direction from volume           | Volume does not give direction | Price structure first                |
| Trusting the volume of one bar          | It looks dramatic              | Compare it with the average          |
| Treating VWAP as a forecast             | The line looks neat            | It is an average, not a target       |
| Always assuming the volume filter works | The rule is simple             | News and session opens are different |

## Pro tips

<Callout type="tip">
  After the breakout, also check volume on the retest. If volume is low on the
  retest and high on the bounce, the strength is holding.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">
  Volume naturally rises at session opens ([Trading
  Sessions](/en/learn/01-foundations/05-trading-sessions)). Consider the time,
  not just the number.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  Low volume does not mean automatic failure. It only means a lack of
  confirmation. No trade without a stop.
</Callout>

## When this does not work

- **Forex tick volume:** it does not always match actual trading.
- **At session opens or on news:** volume is high anyway, so comparisons mislead.
- **Thinly traded assets:** one big order changes the whole picture.
- **On small timeframes:** volume bars are very unstable.

## Summary

<KeyTakeaways>

- Volume shows how many took part; in forex it is tick volume, only an estimate.
- VWAP is the volume-weighted average price.
- Volume clearly above average on a breakout gives confirmation.
- If volume is weak, cut the size or wait.

</KeyTakeaways>
