# Foundations: The Language and Basics of Forex

Do business in a foreign country without speaking the language and you risk being short-changed on every deal. Forex has its own language: pip, lot, spread, margin. This chapter is your first lesson in that language, and the base for the rest of the course.

## What Foundations covers

**Foundations is the set of core ideas about the Forex market: its terms, its costs, its timing and its charts.**

The chapter answers one question: **"What exactly am I buying and selling in Forex, at what cost, when, and how do I read it on a chart?"**

## Why learn this first

- **Every later chapter builds on these terms.** If you don't know what a pip is, you can't understand a [stop](/en/learn/10-trade-management-execution/00-overview) or risk.
- **Wrong terms lead to wrong maths.** Mix up lots and leverage and an account can be gone fast.
- **Costs are a hidden loss.** If you don't know about spread and swap up front, you keep seeing "the trade was right, but I still lost."
- **You need these ideas before choosing a broker and platform.**

## By the end of this chapter you will be able to

- **Explain** the Forex market and currency pairs.
- **Describe** the role of each market participant.
- **Calculate** pips, lots and margin.
- **Work out** the total cost from spread, commission and swap.
- **Convert** session times into your own time zone.
- **Read a chart** and recognise a trend.
- **Choose** a trading style that suits you.

## How the lessons fit together

The order in which a new trader thinks:

1. **What am I trading?** EURUSD, base and quote (Lesson 1).
2. **Who else is in the market?** The big players, and my size (Lesson 2).
3. **How big, and how far?** Lot, pip, margin (Lesson 3).
4. **What does it cost?** Spread, commission (Lesson 4).
5. **When?** My trading window (Lesson 5).
6. **What am I looking at?** Swings, trend (Lesson 6).
7. **What is my style?** Style (Lesson 7).
