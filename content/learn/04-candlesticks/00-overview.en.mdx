# Candlesticks: Read the Language of Price

If you watch a foreign-language film without subtitles, you understand the pictures but not the story. A chart is the same if you cannot read candles. You see price going up and down, but you cannot tell who is fighting and who is losing. **Candlesticks are the subtitles of price.** By the end of this chapter, you will be able to look at one candle and say what happened there.

## What a candlestick is

**A candlestick is a shape that shows the open, high, low and close prices of a set period together.** One H1 candle is the whole story of one hour.

The whole chapter looks for the answer to this question: **"What is this candle saying, and does where it says it matter?"**

## Why learn this now

- **The timing of an entry comes from a candle.** Support, trend or indicators all end up as a decision on one candle.
- **Memorising pattern names is easy, but using them is hard.** This chapter teaches when a pattern means something and when it does not.
- **It keeps you away from false signals.** A candle of the same shape says different things in different places.
- **The later chapters build on this.** Candle confirmation will come up again and again in [Support and Resistance](/en/learn/05-support-resistance/01-levels-vs-zones) and entry strategies.

## By the end of this chapter you will be able to

- **Recognise** a candle's body, wick, open and close.
- **Read** the strength of buyers and sellers from one candle.
- **Tell apart** single-candle patterns such as the Doji, Hammer and Shooting Star.
- **Mark** patterns made of two candles (Engulfing, Harami and so on).
- **Explain** when a Bullish Engulfing works and when it does not.
- **Ignore or give weight to** a candle by looking at its context.

## How these lessons work together

Say a candle catches your eye on the chart. The questions come in this order:

1. **Structure (Lesson 1):** What are the body and wicks like? Who is stronger?
2. **Shape (Lesson 2):** Is it a known single-candle pattern?
3. **Pair (Lessons 3 and 4):** Together with the previous candle, has it formed something bigger?
4. **Context (Lesson 5):** Did it happen in the right place, or does it just look nice?

## What this chapter does not cover

- **Larger patterns of 3 or more candles:** Morning Star, Three White Soldiers and others are in the [Pattern Library](/en/patterns).
- **Support and resistance:** in a separate chapter (Support and Resistance).
- **Entries and risk management:** in the [Risk Management](/en/learn/08-risk-management/01-risk-per-trade) chapter.

## How to use this chapter

- **Look only at candles first, with indicators turned off.** That trains your eye.
- **Start on H1 or H4.** On small timeframes, candle shapes are more random.
- **In each lesson, find 10 examples on your own chart.** Reading and recognising are not the same.
- **Practise on a demo account.**
