# Confirmation vs Trigger: When to Enter Even if the Idea Is Right

Price reached your demand zone. You bought, and price went down through the zone. The idea may have been right, but the timing was wrong.

## What you will be able to do

- **Separate** setup, confirmation and trigger.
- **Recognize** a confirmation candle and **set the trigger price**.
- **Explain the risk** of entering the moment price touches a zone.

## Why it matters

- **A zone is only an area of interest.** Price can turn there, or it can go through.
- **Waiting cuts risk, and does not cut profit much.** Even if you enter a little late, the chance of a loss at the stop is lower.
- **When the trigger is a rule, the decision becomes mechanical.** Then emotion has less to do.

Prerequisites: [Candle Context](/en/learn/04-candlesticks/05-candle-context) and [Support and Resistance](/en/learn/05-support-resistance/01-levels-vs-zones).

## What are setup, confirmation and trigger

**The setup is where price has arrived (the place). Confirmation is the proof that price is turning there (a candle, a rejection). The trigger is the specific condition to enter (for example, breaking the high of the confirmation candle).**

Think of a traffic signal. Reaching the signal is the setup, the yellow light is the confirmation, and the green light is the trigger. Do not speed off just because you see yellow.

| Step         | Question | Example                                             |
| :----------- | :------- | :-------------------------------------------------- |
| Setup        | Where?   | demand zone 1.0860-1.0880                           |
| Confirmation | What?    | bullish engulfing candle                            |
| Trigger      | When?    | buy when the high of the confirmation candle breaks |

## Step by step: the trigger entry

<Steps>

### Step 1: Mark the setup

Draw the [demand zone](/en/learn/05-support-resistance/03-supply-demand). If price does not reach it, wait.

### Step 2: Wait for confirmation

A [bullish engulfing](/en/learn/04-candlesticks/04-bullish-engulfing) in the zone, or a rejection candle with a long lower wick.

### Step 3: Set the trigger price

A pending buy stop a little above the high of the confirmation candle, or a level in your mind. Entry is when this price is passed.

### Step 4: Place the stop

Below the zone and the low of the confirmation candle. Measure the ratio, then the lot.

### Step 5: Cancel if the trigger does not come

The high has not broken even after a few candles? If price goes the other way, the idea is cancelled.

</Steps>

- **Lesson:** entry came only after getting the answers to three questions (where, what, when).

## Common mistakes

| Mistake                              | Why it happens          | What to do                                             |
| :----------------------------------- | :---------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------- |
| Entering on the touch alone          | Greed for a good price  | Wait for confirmation                                  |
| Treating confirmation as the trigger | The candle looks nice   | The trigger is a separate condition: the high breaking |
| Not setting the trigger price        | Unclear rules           | Write it down beforehand                               |
| Pushing a fake confirmation          | Every candle looks good | Check against context (zone, trend)                    |

## Pro tips

<Callout type="tip">
  If the confirmation is weak (small body, big upper wick), cancel the trade
  instead of waiting for the trigger. Entering on an "almost good" candle is not
  a habit to build.
</Callout>

<Callout type="note">
  Waiting for the trigger means entering at a slightly worse price. In exchange
  the risk falls. Accept this trade-off knowingly.
</Callout>

## When this does not work

- **In a fast market:** price can run right after the trigger, and there is slippage.
- **If the confirmation is unclear:** the trigger is unclear too.
- **When the zone breaks:** confirmation came, but the zone failed, so the stop is hit.
- **If the spread is high:** the distance between the trigger and the stop is distorted.

## Summary

<KeyTakeaways>

- Setup (where), confirmation (what), trigger (when): all three are separate.
- Touching a zone is not a reason to enter. It is only an area of interest.
- Write the trigger in advance, such as the high of the confirmation candle breaking.
- If the trigger does not come, cancel the trade.

</KeyTakeaways>

## Practice

Look at price behavior in three demand or supply zones. In each, compare whether confirmation came, at what price the trigger would have been, and what the result would have been if you had entered on the touch.
