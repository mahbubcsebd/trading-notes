## Market Psychology & Institutional Order Flow
After an extended decline, buyers test the upside by aggressively driving prices higher, leaving a tall upper wick. Even though sellers pushed price back near the open, the test proved that significant demand exists at this level. Confirmation on the next bar unleashes the full reversal.

## 5 Strict Anatomy & Recognition Rules
1. **Downtrend Context:** Must form at the bottom of a downtrend or at a defined support floor.
2. **Upper Wick:** Upper shadow must be at least twice the height of the real body.
3. **Lower Wick:** Little to no lower shadow (< 10% of total candle height).
4. **Body Position:** The real body is located in the bottom third of the candle range.
5. **Bullish Confirmation:** The subsequent candle must close green above the Inverted Hammer body.

## 3 Proven Trade Execution Strategies
**Strategy 1 (Break of High Confirmation):** Enter Buy Stop 2 pips above the high of the Inverted Hammer with Stop Loss below the low.
**Strategy 2 (Retracement Limit Entry):** Buy the 50% pullback of the candle on a 15-minute timeframe for tighter risk.
**Strategy 3 (Demand Zone Confluence):** Wait for price to touch the lower support boundary and enter when the hammer closes.

## Where It Works vs Where It Fails
- ✅ **A+ High Probability Setups:** Daily support floor, Sell-Side Liquidity (SSL) sweep, and RSI Bullish divergence.
- ❌ **Low Probability Traps (AVOID):** Forming near resistance (where it becomes a Shooting Star) or during strong downtrend momentum.

> ⚠️ **Pro Risk Management & Mindset Rule:** Always wait for the next candle to confirm that buyers are ready to follow through. Always limit risk to 1%–2% per trade and maintain a predefined Stop Loss.
