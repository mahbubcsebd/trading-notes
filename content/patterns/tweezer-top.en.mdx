## Market Psychology & Institutional Order Flow
Buyers test resistance twice in succession and hit an insurmountable institutional sell wall at the exact same price. The failure to make a higher high triggers profit-taking and aggressive short entries, turning the trend downward rapidly.

## 5 Strict Anatomy & Recognition Rules
1. **Matching Highs:** Identical or near-identical peak prices on both candles.
2. **Candle 1 Bullish:** Green candle maintaining previous upside momentum.
3. **Candle 2 Bearish:** Rejects the same high and closes decisively red.
4. **Resistance Location:** Must occur at a confirmed supply zone or resistance barrier.
5. **Upper Shadow:** Prominent upper wicks demonstrating price rejection.

## 3 Proven Trade Execution Strategies
**Strategy 1 (Candle 2 Close Short):** Sell immediately upon the close of the second bearish candle.
**Strategy 2 (Break of Pattern Low):** Sell stop below the lowest low of the tweezer formation.
**Strategy 3 (Equilibrium Retracement):** Place sell limit at the 50% retracement of Candle 2.

## Where It Works vs Where It Fails
- ✅ **A+ High Probability Setups:** Daily supply zone, Asian High liquidity grab, and Bearish RSI divergence.
- ❌ **Low Probability Traps (AVOID):** Counter-trading strong macro fundamental tailwinds.

> ⚠️ **Pro Risk Management & Mindset Rule:** A tweezer top with long upper shadows provides one of the cleanest stop-loss placements in technical analysis. Always limit risk to 1%–2% per trade and maintain a predefined Stop Loss.
