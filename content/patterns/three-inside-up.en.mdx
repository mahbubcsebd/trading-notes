You saw a bullish harami and waited, because you know a harami is only a pause. The next candle closed above the mother's open: now it is confirmed! You buy. Price rises, right up to a supply zone on the daily chart that turned price away only a week ago. The H4 story was perfect, but the daily chart was telling a different one.

## At a glance

| Item          | Three Inside Up                                                                                                                  |
| :------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Type          | Bullish reversal, three candles (harami + confirmation)                                                                          |
| Where to look | At the end of a downtrend, at a marked support                                                                                   |
| Key features  | 1: long bearish mother; 2: a small candle inside the mother's body (bullish harami); 3: bullish, closing above the mother's open |
| Confirmation  | The third candle is the confirmation                                                                                             |
| Stop          | Below the mother's low, plus spread and a buffer                                                                                 |
| Invalidation  | A close below the mother's low                                                                                                   |
| Look-alikes   | [Bullish Harami](/en/patterns/bullish-harami) (the first two candles), [Morning Star](/en/patterns/morning-star)                 |
| Opposite      | [Three Inside Down](/en/patterns/three-inside-down)                                                                              |

## What you will be able to do

- **Explain** why three inside up is **stronger** than a harami.
- **Separate** it from a morning star.
- **Place** an entry at the third close and a stop below the mother's low.
- **Measure** higher-timeframe zones in advance to filter trades.

## Why it matters

- **It solves the harami's problem.** A harami is only a pause; its first obstacle is the mother's open. Three inside up means that obstacle has already been cleared.
- **Confirmation is built in.** No separate wait for confirmation.
- **The entry comes late, so there is less room.** At the third close price has already risen a lot. Without measuring obstacles above, especially higher-timeframe ones, the R:R misleads you.

Prerequisites: the Bullish Harami page (linked in the table above) and [Multi-Timeframe Analysis](/en/learn/03-market-structure/05-multi-timeframe-analysis).

## What is three inside up

**Three inside up is a three-candle pattern: a long bearish candle, a small candle inside its body (a bullish harami), then a bullish candle that closes above the first candle's open.** "Inside" because the second candle sits within the first; "up" because the third breaks out above.

### Identification rules

| Rule                                                      | Required?               | Why                                               |
| :-------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------- | :------------------------------------------------ |
| A downtrend before it; the mother long and bearish        | Yes                     | The harami's foundation                           |
| The second candle's body inside the mother's body         | Yes                     | This is the harami                                |
| The third candle bullish, closing above the mother's open | Yes                     | This is the "up": the first obstacle cleared      |
| At a marked support                                       | Yes                     | Without a level it is a bounce inside a downtrend |
| Higher-timeframe obstacles at least 1.5R away             | No (but very important) | The entry is late, so there is less room above    |

<Callout type="note">
  If the third candle only clears the middle of the mother body, that confirms
  the harami, but it is not three inside up. Three inside up needs the mother's
  **open** to be cleared.
</Callout>

### Same look, different meaning

| Pattern             | Second candle                              | Third candle                         |
| :------------------ | :----------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------- |
| **Three Inside Up** | **Inside** the mother's body               | Closes above the mother's open       |
| **Morning Star**    | Near or **below** the first candle's close | Closes above 50% of the first body   |
| **Bullish Harami**  | Inside the mother's body                   | Not there yet (a two-candle pattern) |

## The story inside the candle

One common interpretation of three inside up at support:

1. **Mother: sellers push hard.** A long red candle down to support.
2. **Harami: sellers stop.** No new low, a small candle inside the mother.
3. **Third: buyers wipe out the mother's gains entirely.** A close above the mother's open means everyone who sold during the mother candle is now losing.

The result: across three candles, sellers attack, stall and give it all back. But these three candles do not tell you where larger sellers are waiting on the higher timeframe.

<Callout type="note">
  This is a common interpretation, not a proven fact. A chart does not show who
  bought or sold.
</Callout>

## Gaps in a 24-hour market

<Callout type="note">
  As with the harami: in Forex the second candle usually opens at the mother's
  close, so its body starts at the edge of the mother's body. The third starts
  from the second's close and clears the mother's open. No gap is needed.
</Callout>

## Step by step: trading three inside up

<Steps>

### Step 1: Level first, then the higher timeframe

Draw support in advance. Then open one timeframe higher (D1 if you trade H4): is there a supply zone or resistance above? How far away?

### Step 2: Wait for all three candles to close

The third candle can still fall back below the mother's open in its last minute.

### Step 3: Measure

Is the mother long? Is the second candle's body small and inside? Does the third close above the mother's open, near its high?

### Step 4: Entry type

| Entry                           | Where                                  | Pro                            | Con                           |
| :------------------------------ | :------------------------------------- | :----------------------------- | :---------------------------- |
| **Third close** (standard)      | Buy as the third candle closes         | Confirmation is in the pattern | Higher entry, wider stop      |
| **Retest of the mother's open** | Buy limit at the mother's open         | Smaller stop, better R:R       | Missed if price never returns |
| **Third high break**            | Buy stop above the third candle's high | Momentum confirmed             | Even higher entry             |

### Step 5: Place the stop

Below the mother's low (or the second candle's low if it is lower), plus the [spread](/en/learn/01-foundations/04-spread-commission-swap) and a buffer.

### Step 6: Size and target

[Position size](/en/learn/08-risk-management/02-position-sizing) from the entry-to-stop distance, risking no more than 1%. Target before the nearest obstacle, **including higher-timeframe zones**. [Risk reward](/en/learn/08-risk-management/03-risk-reward) at least 1:1.5.

### Step 7: Manage

Repeated closes back below the mother's open mean the confirmation is weakening. A close below the mother's low means the idea is wrong.

</Steps>

### Pre-trade checklist

<Checklist
  id="pattern-three-inside-up-pretrade-en"
  items={[
    "Was support marked before the pattern, and did the mother stop there?",
    "Does the shape pass the rules (long red mother, small harami inside, third closing above the mother's open)?",
    "Have all three candles closed?",
    "Is the risk reward to the nearest obstacle at least 1:1.5?",
    "Is there no zone or level one timeframe up right in front of the trade?",
  ]}
/>

If any box stays empty, skip the trade.

## Example 1: when it works (illustrative)

<Chart
  id="three-inside-up-win-01"
  height={420}
  caption="XAUUSD H1 (illustrative, synthetic) — three inside up at a once-tested support; long at the third close."
/>

- **What was visible:** Candle 4 marked support near 2332.44. Candles 5-10 rallied to about 2387, and 11-15 fell back.
- **The pattern:** Candle 16 was the mother: 2344.44 to 2333.94. Candle 17 was the harami: 2333.94 to 2338.66, inside the body. Candle 18 closed at 2346.94, above the mother's 2344.44 open.
- **Decision:** Buy at the third close at 2346.94. Stop 2331.44 (below the mother's low): $15.50 of risk. Target 2377.94 (2R); room to the swing high was about 2.6R, with no higher-timeframe zone above.
- **Result:** Candle 27 reached the target.
- **Lesson:** A level, a harami, the mother's open cleared, and open space above.

## Example 2: when it is a trap (illustrative)

<Chart
  id="three-inside-up-fail-01"
  height={420}
  caption="GBPUSD H4 (illustrative, synthetic) — a clean three inside up, but a daily supply zone right above. The long reaches the zone, is rejected, and is stopped out."
/>

- **What was visible:** Candles 0-5 rallied into a daily supply zone at about 1.2899-1.2929 and were rejected. Candles 6-8 fell.
- **The pattern:** Candle 9 was the mother: 1.2836 to 1.2808. Candle 10 was the harami: 1.2808 to 1.28206. Candle 11 closed at 1.2840, above the mother's open. Perfect on H4.
- **The wrong decision:** Buy at the third close at 1.2840, stop 1.2800: 40 pips of risk, target 1.2920 (2R). But the bottom of the daily zone at 1.2899 was only about 1.48R away, and the target lay inside the zone.
- **What happened:** Candle 13 reached the zone, candle 14 was rejected, and candle 18 broke the stop.
- **Why it failed:** A higher-timeframe zone right ahead, and no support either. The first, fourth and fifth checklist boxes would have filtered it out.

**Lesson:** Check the higher timeframe: a daily zone overhead caps the trade. Because the stop was set in advance, the loss stayed at 1R.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake                                                  | Why it happens                            | What to do instead                    |
| :------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------ |
| Looking only at your own timeframe                       | Treating the higher timeframe as optional | Draw the zones one timeframe up first |
| Calling a close past the mother's middle three inside up | Mixing up the rules                       | The mother's **open** must be cleared |
| Stop at the second candle's low                          | Wanting a small stop                      | Below the mother's low                |
| Treating morning star and three inside up as the same    | Not checking where the second candle sits | Inside = inside; below = star         |

## Pro tips

<Callout type="tip">
  If the third-close entry feels too high, place a limit order at the mother's
  open. A broken obstacle is often retested once, and entering there keeps the
  same stop with much less risk. More in [Role
  Reversal](/en/learn/05-support-resistance/02-role-reversal).
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">
  How to draw supply and demand zones: [Supply and
  Demand](/en/learn/05-support-resistance/03-supply-demand). Trading H4, check
  the D1 zones; trading H1, check H4. Always look one step up.
</Callout>

## Summary

<KeyTakeaways>

- Three inside up = a long red mother, a small harami inside it, then a green candle closing above the mother's open.
- It is a confirmed harami: the first obstacle (the mother's open) has already been cleared.
- Enter at the third close or on a retest of the mother's open; stop below the mother's low.
- The entry is late, so measure obstacles above in advance, including higher-timeframe zones.

</KeyTakeaways>
