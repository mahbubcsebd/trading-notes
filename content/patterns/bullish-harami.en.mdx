## Market Psychology & Institutional Order Flow
A sudden contraction of volatility following a strong down-move. The small green candle resting entirely within the mother candle’s body shows seller exhaustion and silent institutional accumulation.

## 5 Strict Anatomy & Recognition Rules
1. **Candle 1 (Mother Bar):** Large, prominent bearish candle in the direction of the downtrend.
2. **Candle 2 (Inside Bar):** Small bullish candle whose real body is completely contained inside Candle 1.
3. **Wick Placement:** Preferably, the wicks of Candle 2 also stay within Candle 1 range.
4. **Support Floor:** Must form at a major support or demand level.
5. **Expansion Trigger:** Breakout of the mother bar establishes directional conviction.

## 3 Proven Trade Execution Strategies
**Strategy 1 (Inside Bar High Breakout):** Place Buy Stop above the high of Candle 2 with Stop Loss below Candle 1.
**Strategy 2 (Mother Bar Breakout - Conservative):** Buy once the high of the Mother Bar is breached on a confirmed close.
**Strategy 3 (Lower Timeframe Retest):** Use an M15 breakout retest to minimize drawdown.

## Where It Works vs Where It Fails
- ✅ **A+ High Probability Setups:** HTF support level, Bullish RSI divergence, and clear market structure shift.
- ❌ **Low Probability Traps (AVOID):** Forming inside choppy consolidations without HTF level confluence.

> ⚠️ **Pro Risk Management & Mindset Rule:** The tight range of the inside bar provides an elite Risk:Reward ratio when played from key levels. Always limit risk to 1%–2% per trade and maintain a predefined Stop Loss.
