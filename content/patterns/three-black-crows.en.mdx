## Market Psychology & Institutional Order Flow
Uncontrollable institutional liquidations. Three consecutive tall red candles opening inside previous bodies and driving prices straight down to their session lows prove complete absence of buyers.

## 5 Strict Anatomy & Recognition Rules
1. **Three Consecutive Red Bars:** Three steady or expanding bearish candles.
2. **Progressive Lower Closes:** Each candle closes below the preceding bar low.
3. **Open Within Prior Body:** Each bar opens within the body of the previous candle.
4. **Tiny Lower Shadows:** Very small lower wicks (< 15%), showing relentless bear dominance.
5. **Volume Surge:** Elevated, expanding bear volume confirming institutional exit.

## 3 Proven Trade Execution Strategies
**Strategy 1 (First Pullback Retest - Recommended):** Sell the shallow pullback to the 50% retracement of Candle 3 with Stop Loss above Candle 2.
**Strategy 2 (Break of Low):** Place a Sell Stop order just below the low of Candle 3.
**Strategy 3 (M15 Order Block Snipe):** Drop to M15 and short the mitigation of the M15 Bearish Order Block.

## Where It Works vs Where It Fails
- ✅ **A+ High Probability Setups:** Breakdowns from distribution ranges, Major HTF resistance rejections, and volume expansion.
- ❌ **Low Probability Traps (AVOID):** Appearing after an already exhausted multi-week downtrend into deep support.

> ⚠️ **Pro Risk Management & Mindset Rule:** Do not chase the low of candle 3. Wait for the relief bounce to enter with optimal Risk:Reward. Always limit risk to 1%–2% per trade and maintain a predefined Stop Loss.
