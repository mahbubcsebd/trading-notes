## Market Psychology & Institutional Order Flow
The purest definition of institutional aggression. One side of the market dominated every single second of the session from open to close with zero opposition. The absolute lack of shadows proves that counter-orders were instantly wiped out.

## 5 Strict Anatomy & Recognition Rules
1. **No Wicks:** Zero upper or lower shadows (or less than 2% negligible wicks).
2. **Massive Real Body:** The candle body is 2 to 3 times larger than recent average candles.
3. **Open Equals Extreme:** In a Bullish Marubozu, Open = Low and Close = High. In Bearish, Open = High and Close = Low.
4. **Structural Breakout:** Commonly shatters key consolidation ranges or support/resistance barriers.
5. **Explosive Volume:** High volume confirming true institutional commitment.

## 3 Proven Trade Execution Strategies
**Strategy 1 (50% Equilibrium Retest - Recommended):** Place a Limit order at the 50% midpoint of the Marubozu body. Stop loss behind the candle extreme.
**Strategy 2 (High/Low Continuation):** Enter on the break of the candle close tick with trend momentum.
**Strategy 3 (Fair Value Gap Mitigation):** Drop to M15 and enter at the FVG created by the massive displacement.

## Where It Works vs Where It Fails
- ✅ **A+ High Probability Setups:** Major structural break, High-impact fundamental volume, and clean trending flow.
- ❌ **Low Probability Traps (AVOID):** Appearing as an exhaustion climax after an overextended trend.

> ⚠️ **Pro Risk Management & Mindset Rule:** The 50% midpoint of a Marubozu candle functions as a dynamic institutional order block. Always limit risk to 1%–2% per trade and maintain a predefined Stop Loss.
