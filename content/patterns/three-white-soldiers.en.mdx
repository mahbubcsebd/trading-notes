## Market Psychology & Institutional Order Flow
Complete, unchallenged institutional dominance. Three consecutive tall green candles opening within the prior body and closing near the highs show relentless buying pressure with zero supply available.

## 5 Strict Anatomy & Recognition Rules
1. **Three Bullish Bars:** Three progressive green candles of consistent or expanding size.
2. **Consecutive Higher Closes:** Each candle establishes a clear new session high.
3. **Open Within Body:** Each candle opens inside the body of the previous bar.
4. **Minimal Upper Wicks:** Less than 15% upper shadow, showing strong closing strength.
5. **Volume Confirmation:** Healthy, sustained trading volume across all three bars.

## 3 Proven Trade Execution Strategies
**Strategy 1 (First Pullback Entry - Recommended):** Wait for a shallow pullback to the high of Candle 2 or the 50% midpoint of Candle 3 to buy.
**Strategy 2 (High Breakout Order):** Buy stop above Candle 3 high with Stop Loss below Candle 2 low.
**Strategy 3 (Order Block Confirmation):** Drop to M15 to catch the Bullish Order Block formed by Candle 3.

## Where It Works vs Where It Fails
- ✅ **A+ High Probability Setups:** Breakouts from accumulation ranges, Daily support reversals, and rising volume.
- ❌ **Low Probability Traps (AVOID):** Appearing deep inside severe overbought conditions near HTF resistance.

> ⚠️ **Pro Risk Management & Mindset Rule:** Avoid FOMO at the top of candle 3. The safest entry is always the first minor retest. Always limit risk to 1%–2% per trade and maintain a predefined Stop Loss.
