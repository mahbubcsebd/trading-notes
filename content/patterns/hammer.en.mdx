## Market Psychology & Institutional Order Flow
Sellers aggressively pummeled price downward during the session. However, institutional buyers stepped in with overwhelming demand, pushing price all the way back up to close near the open. The long lower tail proves that lower prices were rejected completely.

## 5 Strict Anatomy & Recognition Rules
1. **Trend Context:** Must appear following a sustained decline or at major support.
2. **Tail Ratio:** Lower shadow must be at least twice the height of the real body.
3. **Upper Shadow:** Little to no upper wick (< 10% of total range).
4. **Top Placement:** Real body situated at the very top of the session range.
5. **Next Bar Validation:** Subsequent candle must close bullishly above the Hammer body.

## 3 Proven Trade Execution Strategies
**Strategy 1 (Break of Hammer High):** Buy Stop placed 2 pips above the Hammer high with Stop Loss below the tail.
**Strategy 2 (50% Wick Retracement):** Place Buy Limit at the 50% midpoint of the long lower wick.
**Strategy 3 (Lower Timeframe Confirmation):** Drop to M15 and buy the retest of the demand zone created by the pin bar.

## Where It Works vs Where It Fails
- ✅ **A+ High Probability Setups:** Major HTF support, Liquidity sweep of prior lows, and Oversold RSI conditions.
- ❌ **Low Probability Traps (AVOID):** Appearing near resistance (where it functions as a Hanging Man) or during early trend phase.

> ⚠️ **Pro Risk Management & Mindset Rule:** A green hammer body indicates slightly higher buying dominance than a red body, but wick length is paramount. Always limit risk to 1%–2% per trade and maintain a predefined Stop Loss.
