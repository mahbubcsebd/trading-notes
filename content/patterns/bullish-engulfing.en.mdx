## Market Psychology & Institutional Order Flow
Sellers were driving price down into major support, inducing retail breakout shorts. Suddenly, institutional demand absorbs all selling pressure. The large green candle completely engulfing the prior red candle proves buyer dominance and traps short traders into forced liquidations.

## 5 Strict Anatomy & Recognition Rules
1. **Preceding Trend:** Must form after an established downtrend or at key HTF support.
2. **Candle 1 (Bearish Exhaustion):** Small red candle reflecting diminishing selling pressure.
3. **Candle 2 (The Engulfing Thrust):** The green candle real body must completely encompass the real body of Candle 1.
4. **Wick Ratio:** Minimal upper wick (< 20% of total candle height), confirming strong closing conviction.
5. **Volume Expansion:** Candle 2 volume must be visibly higher than preceding candles.

## 3 Proven Trade Execution Strategies
**Strategy 1 (50% Equilibrium Retest - Pro Choice):** Measure Candle 2 and place a Buy Limit at the 50% Fibonacci retracement level with Stop Loss below the low.
**Strategy 2 (Candle Close Market Entry):** Execute a Market Buy immediately on the close of Candle 2.
**Strategy 3 (M15 CHoCH Sniper Entry):** Spot the pattern on H4, drop down to M15, wait for a Change of Character (CHoCH), and buy the Bullish Order Block retest.

## Where It Works vs Where It Fails
- ✅ **A+ High Probability Setups:** Daily support floor, Sell-Side Liquidity (SSL) sweep, London/NY session open, and Bullish RSI divergence.
- ❌ **Low Probability Traps (AVOID):** Middle of a consolidation range or counter-trading an aggressive macro downtrend without HTF alignment.
