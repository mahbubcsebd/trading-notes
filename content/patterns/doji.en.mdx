## Market Psychology & Institutional Order Flow
Buyers attempted to rally and sellers tried to sell off during the session, but neither could sustain control. Price closed right where it opened. Following an extended trend, a Doji marks a loss of momentum and a potential turning point.

## 5 Strict Anatomy & Recognition Rules
1. **Negligible Real Body:** Open and close prices are virtually identical.
2. **Bilateral Shadows:** Visible upper and lower shadows displaying two-way price discovery.
3. **Varieties:** Standard Doji, Long-Legged Doji, Dragonfly Doji (bullish), or Gravestone Doji (bearish).
4. **Context is Key:** Significance multiplies when occurring after a mature trend at HTF boundaries.
5. **Requires Confirmation:** A Doji alone is neutral; the subsequent bar dictates resolution.

## 3 Proven Trade Execution Strategies
**Strategy 1 (Breakout Confirmation):** Enter in the direction of the candle following the Doji with Stop Loss at the opposite extreme of the Doji.
**Strategy 2 (Star Setup Continuation):** Trade the Doji as the pivot component of a Morning Star or Evening Star.
**Strategy 3 (Lower Timeframe Range Breakout):** Treat the Doji as a micro-consolidation and enter upon structural breakout on M15.

## Where It Works vs Where It Fails
- ✅ **A+ High Probability Setups:** HTF S/R boundaries, RSI extremes (> 75 or < 25), and high-volume Doji sessions.
- ❌ **Low Probability Traps (AVOID):** Appearing in low-liquidity holiday sessions or flat consolidations.

> ⚠️ **Pro Risk Management & Mindset Rule:** A Doji is a warning sign, not a trigger. Always wait for the breakout candle before allocating capital. Always limit risk to 1%–2% per trade and maintain a predefined Stop Loss.
