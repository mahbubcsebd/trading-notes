## Market Psychology & Institutional Order Flow
Buyers were pushing price higher into a major resistance area, inducing retail breakout longs. Suddenly, institutional smart money steps in with aggressive selling volume. The large red candle completely engulfing the prior green candle confirms buyer exhaustion and absolute bear dominance.

## 5 Strict Anatomy & Recognition Rules
1. **Preceding Trend:** The pattern must appear after an established uptrend or a corrective pullback into higher timeframe resistance.
2. **Candle 1 (Bullish Exhaustion):** A small-to-medium green candle indicating decelerating momentum.
3. **Candle 2 (The Engulfing Slam):** The bearish candle's real body must completely cover the entire real body of Candle 1.
4. **Wick Ratio:** The bearish candle must have a solid, full body with a minimal lower wick (< 20% of total candle size).
5. **Volume Spike:** Volume on the engulfing candle must be visibly higher than the previous 3–5 candles.

## 3 Profitable Trade Execution Strategies
- **Strategy 1 (50% Equilibrium Retest - Pro Choice):** Measure Candle 2 with Fibonacci and set a Sell Limit at the 50% retracement. Stop Loss 5–10 pips above the high. Target 1:3 to 1:5 Risk:Reward.
- **Strategy 2 (Candle Close Market Entry):** Enter a market sell immediately upon the official close of the engulfing candle.
- **Strategy 3 (M15 CHoCH Sniper Entry):** Spot Bearish Engulfing on H4, drop down to M15, wait for a Change of Character (CHoCH), and sell the retest of the M15 Bearish Order Block with a 10–15 pip tight stop.

## Where It Works vs Where It Fails
- ✅ **A+ High Probability:** Daily/H4 supply zone, liquidity sweep of previous highs (BSL grab), London/NY session open, bearish RSI divergence.
- ❌ **Low Probability Traps:** Middle of a choppy consolidation range, fighting a parabolic HTF bull run, 5 minutes before red folder high-impact news.

> ⚠️ **Golden Risk Management Rule:** Never risk more than 1% to 2% of your account per trade. Always add spread buffer to your Stop Loss above the candle high.
