A green candle, then a small red candle inside it. A perfect bearish harami by shape, so you go short. But the rest of the chart would have told you price had been circling one area for ten candles. There was no uptrend; what was there to reverse? This page is the mirror of [Bullish Harami](/en/patterns/bullish-harami); the definition, the inside bar comparison and the gap explanation are detailed there. Only what differs is here.

## At a glance

| Item          | Bearish Harami                                                                                         |
| :------------ | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Type          | Bearish reversal lean, two candles (weak without confirmation)                                         |
| Where to look | At the end of an uptrend, at a marked resistance                                                       |
| Key features  | A long bullish "mother" candle, then a small candle whose body sits inside the mother's body           |
| Confirmation  | The next candle falls or closes below the middle (50%) of the mother body                              |
| Stop          | Above the mother candle's high, plus spread and a buffer                                               |
| Invalidation  | A close above the mother's high                                                                        |
| Look-alikes   | [Inside Bar](/en/patterns/inside-bar), bearish harami cross, [Evening Star](/en/patterns/evening-star) |
| Opposite      | Bullish Harami                                                                                         |

## What you will be able to do

- **Recognise** a bearish harami and a harami cross.
- **Check** whether there is a trend to reverse before trading a reversal pattern.
- **Set** the entry on the mother's midpoint break and the first obstacle at the mother's open.

## Why it matters

- **The picture of an uptrend running out of breath.** A small candle after a big green one means buyers can no longer lift price as before.
- **Nearly meaningless in a range.** Without a trend to reverse, a harami is just two random candles.
- **A harami cross at a top matters more.** Nison's observation: dojis matter more at tops, and the harami cross places that doji inside the mother.

Prerequisites: the Bullish Harami page (linked above) and [Trend, Range and HH-HL](/en/learn/03-market-structure/02-trend-range-hhhl).

## What is a bearish harami

**A bearish harami is a two-candle pattern: a long bullish candle in an uptrend, then a small candle whose body sits entirely inside the previous body.** If the small candle is a doji, it is a **bearish harami cross**.

### Identification rules

| Rule                                                        | Required? | Why                                                       |
| :---------------------------------------------------------- | :-------- | :-------------------------------------------------------- |
| A clear uptrend before it (higher highs, higher lows)       | Yes       | Without a trend to reverse, there is no reversal question |
| The mother candle long and bullish                          | Yes       | Sitting inside a small mother proves nothing              |
| The second candle's body small and inside the mother's body | Yes       | This is the "harami"                                      |
| At a marked resistance                                      | Yes       | Without a level it is a rest inside an uptrend            |
| Second candle is a doji (harami cross)                      | No        | A clearer indecision                                      |

| Quality check    | Strong                                  | Weak                                     |
| :--------------- | :-------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------- |
| What came before | A clear uptrend                         | A sideways range or random candles       |
| Location         | At a marked old high or supply zone     | Inside a range or right after a breakout |
| Second candle    | A doji or very small                    | Nearly half the mother's size            |
| Room below       | 1.5R+ to the mother's open or swing low | The range floor close by                 |

## The story inside the candle

The bullish harami story in reverse. One common interpretation: in the long green mother, buyers lift hard. The next candle barely makes a new high and turns in a small range. Buyers have stopped; sellers have not attacked yet. If the next candle falls below the middle of the mother body, sellers score their first win.

<Callout type="note">
  This is a common interpretation, not a proven fact. A chart does not show who
  bought or sold.
</Callout>

## Gaps in a 24-hour market

<Callout type="note">
  As with the bullish harami: in Forex the second candle usually opens at the
  mother's close. The practical rule: **the second candle opens near the
  mother's close, makes no new high (or only a slight one), and closes inside
  the mother's body.**
</Callout>

## Step by step: trading a bearish harami

The full steps are on the Bullish Harami page, reversed. Only what differs:

<Steps>

### Step 1: Check the trend first

Are there higher highs and higher lows over the last 10-20 candles? If not, stop here. More in [Swing High and Low](/en/learn/03-market-structure/01-swing-high-low).

### Step 2: Two lines

The middle of the mother body (entry trigger) and the mother's open (first obstacle).

### Step 3: Entry and stop

Sell stop just below the mother's middle. Stop above the mother's high, plus the [spread](/en/learn/01-foundations/04-spread-commission-swap). If the [risk reward](/en/learn/08-risk-management/03-risk-reward) to the mother's open is small, take a partial there, or skip the trade if there is not 1.5R to the next swing low.

</Steps>

### Pre-trade checklist

<Checklist
  id="pattern-bearish-harami-pretrade-en"
  items={[
    "Was there a clear uptrend before it, and was resistance marked in advance?",
    "Does the shape pass the rules (long green mother, small body inside its body)?",
    "Have both candles closed, and is the entry trigger below the middle of the mother body?",
    "Is the risk reward to the swing low or obstacle below at least 1:1.5?",
    "Is the higher-timeframe trend or level not against the trade?",
  ]}
/>

If any box stays empty, skip the trade.

## Example 1: when it works (illustrative)

<Chart
  id="bearish-harami-win-01"
  height={420}
  caption="GBPUSD H4 (illustrative, synthetic) — a bearish harami cross at an old high; short below the mother's midpoint."
/>

- **What was visible:** Candle 5 left an old high at 1.2582. Candles 6-10 fell to about 1.2481, and 11-14 rallied again.
- **The harami cross:** Candle 15 was the mother: 1.2542 to 1.2580, a 38-pip body, high 1.2584. Candle 16 opened at 1.2580 and closed at 1.25795: a doji near the top of the mother's body.
- **Decision:** The mother's middle was 1.2561. Sell stop at 1.2559, stop at 1.2589 (above the mother's high): 30 pips of risk. Target 1.2499 (2R); room to the swing low was about 2.6R.
- **Result:** Candle 17 broke through the middle and filled the order. Candle 21 reached the target.
- **Lesson:** A prior trend, a level, a doji harami cross, and an entry after the middle broke.

## Example 2: when it is a trap (illustrative)

<Chart
  id="bearish-harami-fail-01"
  height={420}
  caption="EURUSD H1 (illustrative, synthetic) — a bearish harami in a sideways range, no trend to reverse, then stopped out."
/>

- **What was visible:** Candles 0-8 circled between about 1.0892 and 1.0924. No series of higher highs or lower lows.
- **The harami:** Candle 9 was a green mother (1.0914 to 1.0934), up to the top of the range. Candle 10 was a small red candle (1.0934 to 1.0927) inside its body.
- **The wrong decision:** "Perfect shape." Sell stop 1.0923 (below the middle), stop 1.0940: 17 pips of risk.
- **What happened:** Candle 11 filled the order, 12 rose again, and 13 broke the stop.
- **Why it failed:** There was no uptrend to reverse; the mother was just another swing inside the range, an attempt to break higher if anything. The first checklist box would have filtered it out.

**Lesson:** A reversal pattern needs a trend to reverse. Because the stop was set in advance, the loss stayed at 1R.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake                                       | Why it happens                  | What to do instead                                      |
| :-------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------ |
| Trading a harami in a range                   | Assuming a trend from the shape | Count higher highs and higher lows first                |
| Shorting the harami after a breakout candle   | Reading a pause as a reversal   | A harami after a breakout is often a continuation pause |
| Stop at the small candle's high               | Wanting a small stop            | Above the mother's high                                 |
| Not treating the mother's open as an obstacle | Textbook 2R target              | Take a partial at the mother's open or measure R:R      |

## Pro tips

<Callout type="tip">
  If the candle after the harami closes below the mother's open, the three
  candles often form a **three inside down**, a much stronger signal. More in
  [Three Inside Down](/en/patterns/three-inside-down).
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">
  When a harami follows a range breakout candle, think the other way: it is
  often a rest after the breakout. Then do not short; look for a retest in the
  breakout direction instead.
</Callout>

## Summary

<KeyTakeaways>

- Bearish harami = a long green mother candle, then a small candle whose body sits inside the mother's body.
- Without a clear prior uptrend it is not a reversal pattern, just two candles.
- Enter after the middle of the mother body breaks; stop above the mother's high; first obstacle at the mother's open.
- A harami cross (doji) at a top matters more.

</KeyTakeaways>

## Practice and your own backtest

Open GBPUSD H4 or EURUSD H1 in TradingView. Find at least 20 bearish haramis. For each, log: date, whether there was a prior uptrend (higher highs and higher lows over the last 10 candles), whether it was at resistance, whether it was a harami cross, whether the middle broke, and result (R). Then split into "trend before" and "range before" and compare the average R. Do not draw conclusions from fewer than 30 samples.
