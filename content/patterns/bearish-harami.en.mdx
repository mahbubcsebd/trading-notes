## Market Psychology & Institutional Order Flow
Buyer momentum grinds to a dead stop at the peak of an uptrend. The inability of Candle 2 to create a higher high, closing as a small red bar inside Candle 1, indicates that institutional supply is blocking further advance.

## 5 Strict Anatomy & Recognition Rules
1. **Candle 1 (Mother Bar):** Large bullish candle representing the final surge of the uptrend.
2. **Candle 2 (Inside Bar):** Small red candle whose body is entirely enclosed inside Candle 1.
3. **Range Contraction:** Noticeable decrease in candle size and volatility.
4. **Resistance Level:** Must form at major HTF supply or structural resistance.
5. **Breakdown Confirmation:** The subsequent candle breaks and closes below the Inside Bar low.

## 3 Proven Trade Execution Strategies
**Strategy 1 (Inside Bar Low Sell Stop):** Place a Sell Stop 2 pips below Candle 2 low with Stop Loss above Candle 1 high.
**Strategy 2 (Mother Bar Breakdown - Conservative):** Sell once price decisively closes below Candle 1 low.
**Strategy 3 (Lower Timeframe Retest):** Drop to M15 and sell the retest of the Bearish Order Block created during the consolidation.

## Where It Works vs Where It Fails
- ✅ **A+ High Probability Setups:** Daily supply zone, Asian High liquidity sweep, and Bearish RSI divergence.
- ❌ **Low Probability Traps (AVOID):** Shorting in a runaway macro bull trend without multi-timeframe alignment.

> ⚠️ **Pro Risk Management & Mindset Rule:** Inside bar setups provide ultra-tight stop loss placement for maximum Risk-to-Reward returns. Always limit risk to 1%–2% per trade and maintain a predefined Stop Loss.
