## Market Psychology & Institutional Order Flow
Bears attempt to break through a key price level twice and fail at the identical tick. This double rejection demonstrates strong institutional absorption. Once the second candle closes bullish, short sellers are forced into covering, igniting an explosive upward rally.

## 5 Strict Anatomy & Recognition Rules
1. **Identical Lows:** Both candles share an exact or near-identical lowest price point.
2. **Candle 1 Bearish:** The first bar is a red candle following the downtrend.
3. **Candle 2 Bullish:** The second candle bounces off the same low and closes green.
4. **Key Location:** Must form directly on a recognized HTF support or demand level.
5. **Wick Rejection:** Clear lower wicks displaying rejection of cheaper pricing.

## 3 Proven Trade Execution Strategies
**Strategy 1 (Candle 2 Close Market Buy):** Enter on the closing tick of the second bullish bar. Stop loss 3–5 pips below the twin lows.
**Strategy 2 (Break of Pattern High):** Place Buy Stop above the high of the tweezer formation.
**Strategy 3 (Lower Timeframe Retest):** Drop to M15 and buy the order block retest following the second rejection.

## Where It Works vs Where It Fails
- ✅ **A+ High Probability Setups:** HTF daily support, Previous Day Low (PDL) liquidity sweep, and oversold momentum.
- ❌ **Low Probability Traps (AVOID):** No clean support level or occurring inside mid-range chop.

> ⚠️ **Pro Risk Management & Mindset Rule:** Identical lows create a clear invalidation level, offering an exceptional Risk-to-Reward ratio. Always limit risk to 1%–2% per trade and maintain a predefined Stop Loss.
