## Market Psychology & Institutional Order Flow
At the top of an uptrend, sellers managed to crash price significantly during the session before buyers recovered it. This reveals that underlying institutional supply is emerging. Once the low of this candle is broken, long holders panic and trigger a waterfall sell-off.

## 5 Strict Anatomy & Recognition Rules
1. **Uptrend Peak:** Must appear after a prolonged uptrend or at major resistance.
2. **Lower Wick:** The lower shadow must be at least twice the length of the real body.
3. **Upper Wick:** Negligible or non-existent upper shadow.
4. **Body Position:** Small body situated at the top of the session range.
5. **Mandatory Confirmation:** The following candle must close lower than the Hanging Man.

## 3 Proven Trade Execution Strategies
**Strategy 1 (Break of Low Sell):** Place a Sell Stop order just below the lowest wick of the Hanging Man.
**Strategy 2 (Confirmation Close):** Enter market short only after the next candle closes as a decisive red bar.
**Strategy 3 (Fibonacci Retest):** Sell the 50% retracement of the subsequent breakdown candle.

## Where It Works vs Where It Fails
- ✅ **A+ High Probability Setups:** RSI divergence above 70, Daily resistance level, and London session liquidity purge.
- ❌ **Low Probability Traps (AVOID):** Occurring in an early-stage trend or near key support.

> ⚠️ **Pro Risk Management & Mindset Rule:** Never trade a Hanging Man without waiting for the next candle to confirm the downward breakdown. Always limit risk to 1%–2% per trade and maintain a predefined Stop Loss.
