## Market Psychology & Institutional Order Flow
Bulls push aggressively to new highs on Candle 1. On Candle 2, upside momentum stalls completely, creating a tiny body that traps retail breakout buyers. On Candle 3, institutional smart money dumps liquidity, pushing prices violently downward past the 50% midpoint of Candle 1. Trapped bulls are forced to liquidate into institutional short orders.

## 5 Strict Anatomy & Recognition Rules
1. **Preceding Trend:** Must appear at the peak of an uptrend or at a key HTF resistance level.
2. **Candle 1 (Bullish Expansion):** A tall green candle representing the final push of buyer momentum.
3. **Candle 2 (The Star):** A small-bodied candle (Doji or Spinning Top) showing exhaustion and stalling volume.
4. **Candle 3 (Bearish Reversal):** A strong red candle that closes well past the 50% midpoint of Candle 1.
5. **Volume Confirmation:** Candle 3 must exhibit a clear expansion in trading volume.

## 3 Proven Trade Execution Strategies
**Strategy 1 (50% Equilibrium Retest - Pro Choice):** Measure Candle 3 and place a Sell Limit at the 50% Fibonacci retracement level. Stop Loss goes above the high of Candle 2.
**Strategy 2 (Candle 3 Close Market Order):** Enter a market sell immediately upon the close of Candle 3 with Stop Loss above the star.
**Strategy 3 (Lower Timeframe Sniper Entry):** Spot the pattern on H4, drop down to M15, wait for a Change of Character (CHoCH), and sell the retest with 10–15 pip risk.

## Where It Works vs Where It Fails
- ✅ **A+ High Probability Setups:** Daily/H4 supply zone, Asian High liquidity sweep, Bearish RSI divergence, and London/NY session open.
- ❌ **Low Probability Traps (AVOID):** Middle of a consolidation range or trading against a parabolic macro bull trend.

> ⚠️ **Pro Risk Management & Mindset Rule:** Never front-run Candle 2. The entire statistical edge lies in Candle 3 confirming the institutional shift. Always limit risk to 1%–2% per trade and maintain a predefined Stop Loss.
