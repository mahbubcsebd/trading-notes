## Market Psychology & Institutional Order Flow
Buyers drove prices up to new session highs. However, at peak resistance, institutional smart money heavily offloaded positions, slamming the price back down to the lower range. The extended upper wick exposes complete buyer exhaustion.

## 5 Strict Anatomy & Recognition Rules
1. **Peak Location:** Must occur at the peak of an uptrend or at defined HTF supply.
2. **Upper Shadow:** Upper wick must be at least twice the size of the real body.
3. **Lower Shadow:** Little to no lower shadow (< 10% of candle range).
4. **Body Position:** Body sits strictly at the bottom of the total price range.
5. **Follow-through:** The next candle must confirm with a downward close below the star body.

## 3 Proven Trade Execution Strategies
**Strategy 1 (Break of Low):** Place Sell Stop 2 pips below the Shooting Star low with Stop Loss above the wick.
**Strategy 2 (50% Upper Shadow Retest):** Place Sell Limit at the 50% midpoint of the upper tail.
**Strategy 3 (Order Block Confirmation):** Drop to M15 and short the mitigation of the M15 Bearish Order Block.

## Where It Works vs Where It Fails
- ✅ **A+ High Probability Setups:** Daily resistance level, Buy-Side Liquidity (BSL) grab, and Bearish RSI divergence.
- ❌ **Low Probability Traps (AVOID):** Occurring at a key support floor (where it acts as Inverted Hammer).

> ⚠️ **Pro Risk Management & Mindset Rule:** When a shooting star sweeps liquidity above an old high and snaps back, it is one of the highest win-rate setups in trading. Always limit risk to 1%–2% per trade and maintain a predefined Stop Loss.
