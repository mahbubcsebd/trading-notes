## Market Psychology & Institutional Order Flow
Bears control Candle 1 completely. On Candle 2, downside momentum abruptly stalls into a tight indecision range. On Candle 3, institutional buyers step in aggressively, thrusting the close well above the 50% midpoint of Candle 1. Trapped short sellers are forced into stop-loss buybacks, fueling the rally.

## 5 Strict Anatomy & Recognition Rules
1. **Prior Downtrend:** Must emerge after a sustained downtrend at key support.
2. **Candle 1 (Bearish Impulse):** Long red candle representing the final wave of selling.
3. **Candle 2 (The Star):** Small-bodied candle or Doji highlighting seller deceleration.
4. **Candle 3 (Bullish Surge):** Strong green candle closing decisively above the 50% mark of Candle 1.
5. **Volume Expansion:** Noticeable expansion in buyer volume on Candle 3.

## 3 Proven Trade Execution Strategies
**Strategy 1 (50% Equilibrium Retest - Pro Choice):** Measure Candle 3 and place a Buy Limit at the 50% Fibonacci retracement level with Stop Loss below the star low.
**Strategy 2 (Candle 3 Close Market Buy):** Buy immediately upon the close of Candle 3.
**Strategy 3 (M15 Sniper Entry):** Drop to M15, wait for a Change of Character (CHoCH), and enter on the retest of the Bullish Order Block.

## Where It Works vs Where It Fails
- ✅ **A+ High Probability Setups:** Daily demand zone, Sell-Side Liquidity sweep, and Bullish RSI divergence.
- ❌ **Low Probability Traps (AVOID):** Appearing without HTF support alignment or mid-range consolidation.

> ⚠️ **Pro Risk Management & Mindset Rule:** If the middle star candle is a Doji (Morning Doji Star), the reversal probability jumps significantly. Always limit risk to 1%–2% per trade and maintain a predefined Stop Loss.
