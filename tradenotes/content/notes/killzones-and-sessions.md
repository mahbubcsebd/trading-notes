## Windows

These are approximate and shift by an hour with daylight saving. Times are UTC+6.

| Window | Winter (BD) | Summer (BD) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Asian range | 6 am to 12 pm | 6 am to 12 pm | Builds the range that gets swept later |
| London open | 1 pm to 4 pm | 12 pm to 3 pm | First large move of the European day |
| New York open | 7 pm to 10 pm | 6 pm to 9 pm | Overlaps London, highest volume |

> **Key idea:** I do not need to watch all day. One or two windows I can follow fully is better than a screen glued to me for ten hours.

## Plan

- Mark Asian high and low before London.
- Mark the previous day high and low.
- Decide the bias on the daily and 4H before the window starts.
- Write the plan in the journal, then stop looking for trades outside the window.

## Reminder

No trade is also a valid result. Skipping a messy session protects the account.
