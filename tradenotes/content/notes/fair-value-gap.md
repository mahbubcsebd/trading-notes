## Definition

A **fair value gap** is a three-candle pattern where the wicks of candle 1 and candle 3 do not overlap. The space between them is an imbalance: price moved so fast that one side barely traded there.

- **Bullish FVG:** gap between the high of candle 1 and the low of candle 3.
- **Bearish FVG:** gap between the low of candle 1 and the high of candle 3.

## Why traders watch it

The idea is that price often comes back to rebalance part of the gap before continuing. That makes it a possible entry area in the direction of the move.

> **Key idea:** a gap inside a strong trend is a pullback zone. A gap against the trend, or in the middle of a range, is much weaker.

## Using it

1. Identify the direction from a higher timeframe.
2. Wait for an impulsive move that leaves a gap.
3. Watch for price to return into the gap, often to the 50% level.
4. Look for a lower timeframe confirmation, then place the stop beyond the gap.

## When it tends to fail

- The gap is tiny compared with the average candle size.
- News is about to release.
- Price has already filled the gap completely and closed through it.

## Notes to self

Mark the gap, then ask: *what is the story?* If I cannot say in one sentence why price should react here, I skip it.
