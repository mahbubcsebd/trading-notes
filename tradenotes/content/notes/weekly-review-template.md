## Numbers

- Trades taken, win rate, total R
- Plan followed on how many trades?
- Largest loss and why it happened

## Process

1. Which setup worked best this week? Which cost me the most?
2. Did I trade outside my windows?
3. Did I move a stop or skip one?
4. Which trade would I take again exactly the same way?

## Feelings

Write one line for each: *before a loss*, *after a win*, *when I was flat*. Patterns here often explain more than the chart does.

> **Key idea:** a journal only helps if I read it. Fifteen minutes on Sunday is the habit that matters.

## One change for next week

Pick **one** thing. Not five. Write it at the top of the next week's plan.
