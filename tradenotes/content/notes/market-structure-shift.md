## Break of structure (BOS)

Price breaks a swing high in an uptrend, or a swing low in a downtrend. The trend **continues**.

## Market structure shift (MSS)

Price breaks the swing that was protecting the trend, for example the last higher low in an uptrend. It is the first sign the trend **may be changing**.

> **Key idea:** BOS means continuation. MSS means possible reversal. The same candle can be either depending on the trend I was in.

## Table

| Term | Trend before | What breaks | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BOS | Up | Previous swing high | Continuation |
| BOS | Down | Previous swing low | Continuation |
| MSS | Up | Last higher low | Possible reversal |
| MSS | Down | Last lower high | Possible reversal |

## Rules I use

- Close beyond the level, not just a wick.
- Check it on the timeframe I am trading, then the one above.
- A shift right after a liquidity sweep carries more weight.
